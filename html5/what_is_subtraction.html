What is Subtraction?

Here you will learn what Subtraction is.

The term "subtraction" represents a mathematical operation. Subtraction is an action that you perform using numbers. It allows you to find the difference of 2 numbers.

Use the minus (-) button to express the subtraction operation in the app.

In the subtraction process, you take a number and reduce it to a smaller number; that is, we take away one number from another.
