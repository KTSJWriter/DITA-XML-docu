Access and Roles

Go to MyAccess (go/myaccess) to request the necessary roles.

  • To use Harness: PP_xyz_operator
  • To deploy to Production: PP_xyz_Deployment (you must also have current SRE IPCF Training, previously known as "Think Twice"),

Requests go to your manager for approval and may take 40 minutes to sync. For information about other roles, see Harness.io Roles in MyAccess.

To log in to Harness, go here, click the "Single sign-on" button, and enter your PayPal email address.
